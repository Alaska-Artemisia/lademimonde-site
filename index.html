Demi-monde is an intimates house — lingerie, corsetry, hosiery and robes — launching shortly.

The name is French, 1855. Demi-monde — the half-world. It named the women who lived at the edge of respectable society and were spoken about rather than to.

We mean the other half as well. The one she keeps.
